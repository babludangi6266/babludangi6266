## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Full Stack Developer** with 2+ years of production experience building scalable web platforms on the **MERN stack**, with working proficiency in **Java / Spring Boot**. Over the past year I've been extending that foundation into **applied AI engineering** — designing and shipping **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines**, integrating **LLMs** into production systems, and working hands-on with **vector databases** and semantic search.

I care about writing systems that are simple to reason about, easy to scale, and genuinely useful to the people using them — whether that's a government data platform serving millions of records or an internal tool that saves a team hours every week.

- 🔭 Currently building **Repomind** — an AI-powered codebase Q&A tool for small dev teams (LangChain + Qdrant + tree-sitter based semantic chunking)
- 🌱 Deepening my expertise in **LLM orchestration, vector search, and agentic workflows**
- 💼 Production work spans **agri-data platforms (Govt. of India), fintech, ed-tech, and manufacturing ERP systems**
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on **AI-integrated SaaS products** and **independent/freelance engineering work**
